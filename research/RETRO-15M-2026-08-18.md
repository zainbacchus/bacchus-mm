# Retrospective: Phase D (15-minute markets) concluded, negative

Written 2026-08-18, after the third kill-switch halt and five days of the
bot sitting idle. This is the deliverable the goal doc promised: "if no
configuration measures positive, the project concludes and the repo stands
as the documented map of why." That condition is now met, and unlike every
earlier read, it is met WITH statistical significance.

## The verdict

Passive join-the-touch market making on Kalshi's 15-minute up/down markets,
at 1-contract size across six series, does not clear the goal (2x the
risk-free rate, about 11 cents/day on $500). The final measured session was
negative at 2.45 sigma, the per-contract edge is negative rather than
merely unproven, and two independent arithmetic arguments (below) show the
configuration cannot be rescued by more levers or by more size.

Account: **$525 deposited, $462.08 equity at conclusion = -$62.92 (-12.0%
of capital) over seven trading days**, across about 23,000 contracts and
roughly $13,000 of notional volume. Total fees paid: $0.15 (the zero-maker-
fee finding held all the way through).

## The decisive numbers

Final session (2026-08-12T19:52Z restart to the 2026-08-13T03:07Z halt,
7.25 hours, settled-vs-settlement from a fixed t0):

- **Total: -$30.52 on 622.4 contracts = -4.90 c/contract, se 2.00c, 2.45
  sigma NEGATIVE.** The first significant read the project ever produced in

- **Buys: -13.92 c/ct on 281.1 ct (se 2.98c, ~4.7 sigma).** Sells: +2.52

- **Minute-13 carried -$23.78 of the -$30.52 (78%)** on 200.6 ct = -11.85
  c/ct (se 3.53c, ~3.4 sigma). Minute-13 is the FIRST quotable minute under

- Per series: ETH -10.99, SILVER -9.81, WTI -8.52, GOLD -4.37 c/ct;
  XRP +2.00, BTC +3.28.

Daily settled ledger (research/daily/LEDGER.csv): -7.88, +3.18, +17.80,

Halts: kill switch at 2026-08-11T08:53:56Z, again 2026-08-12T17:53:48Z

initiated stop 2026-08-12T02:46Z during the macro-lean night.

## Three regimes in four days

1. **Window-open snipers (Aug 10-11).** Fresh 15-minute markets open
   anchored near 50c; counterparties watching the spot tape sold into our
   still-anchored bids before the young book repriced. The entire loss lived
   in the first minute (-$23 there while every other minute made +$14.50),
   concentrated where the joined queue was thin. Full forensics:
   ATTRIBUTION-WINDOW-OPEN-2026-08-11.md. Fix: 90-second open delay.
2. **The macro lean (Aug 11-12).** Metals, crypto, and oil drifted upward
   together, so six "diversified" books were one bet; a symmetric maker in a
   leaning tape donates on the short side. Sells -13.1 c/ct at 6.9 sigma
   while buys made +9.5. No jump-based defense could see it because nothing
   jumped. Full forensics: ATTRIBUTION-DRIFT-2026-08-12.md. Fix: M7 side
   tripwire.
3. **The minute-13 migration (Aug 12-13).** Within hours of the delay going
   live, the loss reappeared at the new first-touch minute, on NORMAL queue
   depth (median join depth 86 at minute-13 vs 94 elsewhere; thin-queue
   share 20% vs 23%). The flow followed the posture, not the location.

The pattern that matters: the adversary was not exploiting a *place*, it was
exploiting a *predictable posture* (1-lot, join-the-touch, both sides, every
window). Each fix relocated the seam within hours.

## What was built (and it all worked)

Seven levers, each independently disable-able with its own attribution
telemetry: M1 favorite-longshot tilt (walked 0.90 -> 0.65 on 5-sigma
own-fill evidence), M2 toxicity pull, M3 price-shaped dollar caps, M4
Coinbase spot-jump pull, M5 flow gate, M6 scheduled-release pull, M7
per-side rolling-expectancy tripwire; plus the 90-second open delay, a $30
cumulative-drawdown kill switch, post-only invariants (zero taker fills in
about 23,000 contracts), and exchange-side order groups.

None of this failed. That is the point. On the final night M7 fired three
times (buy -8.31c on 115 ct at 17:10Z, buy -8.20c on 171 ct at 21:24Z, sell
-9.57c on 110 ct at 23:20Z), leaked ZERO fills on a suppressed side, and the
cohort it flagged at -9.57c marked went on to SETTLE at -10.77c: the
detector was right. The account hit the kill switch anyway.

## What was refuted before being built (the loop's best work)

Four counter-levers were killed by data instead of by experience:

1. **Trailing-drift pull.** Parameterized against real fills: losing sells
   sat mostly on FLAT trailing tape; the best threshold saved $1.34 of a
   $10.69 night while cutting a third of volume; the adverse-side variant
   would have suppressed +$7.74 of the night's BEST fills.
2. **Hour-of-day curfew.** Lifetime map over 21,327 fills: US session -0.07
   c/ct vs off-hours +0.03 c/ct, both ~0.1 sigma. Episodic, not clock-bound.
3. **Join-depth posting filter.** Killed by the minute-13 depth data above:
   the losses were not thin-queue.
4. **Per-series spot-jump thresholds (the "raise ZEC's bar" idea).** Pointed
   the wrong way: ZEC's problem was a sustained grind that a 20-second
   jump-cooloff re-armed into; fewer pulls would have bled more.

## The two arithmetic arguments that close it

**1. Protection costs 100-300x the prize.** Any outcome-conditioned breaker
must observe the loss to detect it. M7 cannot fire until a side reaches -8
c/ct on 100 contracts, so a trip costs about $8 by construction ($10-15
observed). Toxic episodes occurred on 4+ of 7 trading days, several per day
by the end, so the detection cost alone runs $10-30/day against an 11
cents/day target. A safety mechanism whose minimum cost is two orders of
magnitude above the profit target is not protecting an edge; its cost is
evidence there is no edge of that size to protect.

**2. The experiment cannot reach significance before the bankroll dies.**
The best surviving candidate is BTC at roughly +0.21 c/ct pooled (about
5,600 contracts, se 0.67c, ~0.3 sigma: NOT established). Resolving an edge
that size at 2 sigma needs se ~0.1c, i.e. about **250,000 contracts** -
6-12 months of continuous running at observed volumes. Over that horizon,
regime episodes at $10-15 apiece and 1-2 per day imply thousands of dollars
of expected drawdown to prove a roughly $40/year edge on a $525 account.
The measurement is off by two orders of magnitude from the capital.

Add the multiple-comparisons exposure - seven levers, several fitted to
single episodes, plus post-hoc bucket searches across minute, side, price
band, depth, hour, and series - and even a future positive read from this
configuration would deserve suspicion.

## What this record does NOT claim

- Not that market making on Kalshi is impossible. Professionals are on these
  books and presumably profitable at their cost structure, latency, and
  size; the Buergi-Deng-Whelan (2026) result that makers on >=50c contracts
  earn +2.6% after fees stands unchallenged by anything here.
- Not that every measured pocket is noise. BTC and the 0.65-0.90 favorites
  band were persistently the least-bad cuts. They are hypotheses, not
  edges, and this account cannot afford to test them.
- Not that the levers were wrong. Each was justified by its evidence at the
  time and each did what it claimed. The negative result is about the
  posture they were defending, not about the defenses.

## What a future attempt would need

Not another lever. A structurally different posture, pre-registered with its
own budget and kill criterion. The single candidate the data hints at:
**stop joining the touch.** Rest 2-3 ticks behind the best price and accept
a small fraction of the fills, so that captured spread exceeds adverse
selection instead of racing it. That is a different bet with a different
failure mode (starvation rather than selection), and the calm-MM era's
0.26% fill rate at distance says it is a long shot. It should be a new
project, not a continuation of this one.

Other honest prerequisites: colocation in us-east-2 (Kalshi's matching
engine region) if latency ever proves to matter, which this repo's own
latency budget says it currently does not; and a bankroll sized so that the
significance horizon and the drawdown regime are compatible - roughly, if a
protective breaker costs $10 per episode and episodes are daily, the account
needs to survive months of that while the edge accumulates.

## What survives

The strategy is retired. The apparatus is the product, and it is reusable:

- Rich, replayable logging (every quote decision with book state, inventory,
  queue depth, and lever flags) plus a SQLite mirror.
- research/daily_review.py: deterministic read-only settled-PnL review with
  the measurement rules encoded (fixed t0, unsegmented, verified sign
  convention, balance anchoring), an accounting ledger, and a chart.
- research/pickoff_report.py: settled money vs post-fill markout, bucketed
  by series, minute, side, price band, and queue depth. This is the tool
  that named every regime.
- A daily cloud review routine that opens evidence-quoted PRs, and a
  six-hourly pulse that stays silent when healthy and files an issue when
  fills stall or drawdown outruns the cadence. The pulse caught the Aug 12
  drawdown 28 minutes before the owner did.
- Two prior retrospectives and three attribution documents, so the next
  attempt starts from measured facts rather than folklore.
