**This is the loudest number in today's pull and it deserves emphasis.**
Buying the cheap side (0.10-0.35c, effectively the longshot on whichever
market) lost -7.29c/ct on 1320 contracts; buying the expensive side
(0.65-0.90c, the favorite) gained +7.83c/ct on 1298 contracts. Both are
roughly 5+ standard errors from zero, an order of magnitude larger and more

almost symmetric (net effect on total PnL nets out, consistent with the
flat headline).

This looks like the same favorite-longshot mechanism M1 was calibrated on
(favorites underpriced, longshots overpriced), but showing up much more
broadly across the price axis than the original calibration window
(favorites at 0.98-1.00, late-window, +0.57c/ct, ~4 sigma) covered.
`tilt_tail_threshold: 0.90` only engages M1 above 0.90 / below 0.10, so
none of this 0.10-0.35 / 0.65-0.90 signal is currently being acted on by
that lever.

I am **not** proposing a `tilt_tail_threshold` change from this cut, for

the live fly DB, not available to this read-only, DB-less routine; (2) this

review too. But given the size of the effect (5+se, ~1300 contracts per
